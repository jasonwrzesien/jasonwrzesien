# Jason Wrzesien

**`Software Development/Leadership/Marketing`**

I'm a Computer Science student at the Georgia Institute of Technology specializing in Cyber Security and Information Internetworks. With a solid academic foundation and hands-on experience, I am proficient in a variety of programming languages. In my free time I enjoy working through complex programming problems and learning new languages. Using my expertise in web development and market research, I co-founded a startup called Quuri: a web application used as a platform for researchers to network, collaborate and publicize research. I’ve shipped live product features while as an intern at Qu, granting me experience in a fast-paced development environment- including the usage of sprint planning, code reviews, and stand-up meetings.

Outside of academics, I enjoy a wide range of hobbies, but the one I am most proud of would have to be cooking. Learning most of my cooking skills through my mom and online networks like YouTube for FoodNetwork, I am confident in being able to cook most cuisines to an intermediate level- though if I had to choose one, I have taken most inspiration from French culinary techniques and styles. I am also involved in many sports, being a part of swimming and tennis for 10+ years while also playing basketball, baseball, ultimate frisbee, pickleball, and most recently, golf.
